GET /api/diagnostics/device is the form .forge/PREFLIGHT.md already specified
and it was a 404. It is unauthenticated because the four preflight lines curl it
with no session. The values come from the browser, posted once per launch to
POST /api/diagnostics/launch, and are persisted through Drizzle in the
device_observation table, migration drizzle/0001_diagnostics.sql.

Headless Chrome on this Mac is not a Home Screen launch, so standaloneSeen is
false and lastReportedAt.standaloneSeen carries the time it said so. Wake Lock is
only believed from a launch that was standalone, so a desktop tab cannot flip it:
the preflight line asks about the installed app on the phone. passkeyRegistered
and pushSubscription have no source until slices 2 and 12 and read false because
nothing has been observed, which is the truth.
